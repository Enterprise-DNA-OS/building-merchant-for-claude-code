# Rule checks for a timber and building supplies merchant

Checked 30 October 2026. Run `npm run merchant -- compliance` (or `/compliance`, `/treatment-check`). These checks find quotes, orders, accounts, products and invoices whose records look inconsistent with the rules below. They check what is recorded, not the timber, the site or the contract, and they do not replace your own reading of the standards or legal advice. The `jurisdiction` setting in `merchant.settings` (NZ or AU) picks the hazard classes and the New Zealand-only rules. The full standards (NZS 3602, NZS 3640, NZS 3604, AS 1604.1) are sold by Standards New Zealand and Standards Australia; the building consent authority has the final say on what is acceptable on a given job.

| Check | What the data shows | Rule and source | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| treatment-for-use | An open quote or order line of treated timber is below the hazard class its recorded end use needs (H3.1 boards quoted as decking, H1.2 framing ordered for deck joists, H4 posts quoted for a retaining wall). | New Zealand: Building Code clause B2 Durability, Acceptable Solution B2/AS1, which cites NZS 3602:2003 for the treatment each timber use needs; hazard classes are defined in NZS 3640:2003. BRANZ summarises the B2/AS1 table: H1.2 for enclosed framing, H3.1 for cavity battens, H3.2 for decking, deck joists and bearers, exposed subfloor framing, fence rails and palings and cantilevered joists, H4 for fence posts, H5 for piles, poles and posts in the ground. [BRANZ timber treatment](https://www.branz.co.nz/design-build/articles/timber-treatment), [NZS 3640 on Codehub](https://codehub.building.govt.nz/resources/36402003nzs), [Standards NZ notice on H3.1 for framing](https://www.standards.govt.nz/news-and-updates/changes-to-h3-1-for-solid-timber-framing-notice). Australia: hazard classes H1 to H6 under AS 1604.1. [WoodSolutions use classes](https://www.woodsolutions.com.au/publications/preservative-treatments-and-use-classes) | The end use table is `merchant.end_uses`, which the operator owns. A line with no end use cannot be checked (see end-use-unknown). Species with natural durability, and treatment other than for Pinus, need the standard itself. |
| end-use-unknown | Treated timber on an open quote or order with no end use, or an end use not in the table. | Operator policy in this base: every treated timber line carries its end use so the treatment can be checked before it ships. | A warning, not a breach. |
| grade-for-structure | Timber on a structural end use (framing, deck framing, cantilevered joists) with no stress grade, or a non-structural grade such as Utility. | NZS 3604:2011 timber-framed buildings designs to stress-graded timber (SG8 and above); NZS 3602:2003 and B2/AS1 set the treatment. Australia: structural framing to an F-grade or MGP grade. | Reads the recorded grade only (SG, MSG, MGP, F or LVL followed by a number). It does not check sizes or spans. |
| ppsr-missing | A trade account has a credit limit and no security interest registered against it. | New Zealand: Personal Property Securities Act 1999. A retention of title clause in your credit terms protects you against other creditors only once a financing statement is registered on the PPSR. Australia: Personal Property Securities Act 2009 (Cth), registration on the Australian PPSR. [PPSR financing statements](https://ppsr.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/registering-on-the-ppsr/about-financing-statements/) | Records the registration date you enter. It does not search the register. |
| ppsr-expiring | The registration has lapsed, or lapses within `ppsr_warn_days` (90 by default). | New Zealand: a financing statement expires five years after registration unless renewed, and the PPSR does not remind you (Personal Property Securities Act 1999, s 153 and s 154). [Managing your financing statements](https://ppsr.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/registering-on-the-ppsr/managing-your-financing-statements/) | Australia: the end time is the one chosen at registration; record it as the expiry date. |
| credit-application | A trade account has a credit limit and no signed credit application on file. | Operator policy in this base: no credit without signed terms (these carry the retention of title clause the registration relies on). | Change it in a migration if your policy differs. |
| guarantee | A trade account has credit and signed terms, but no personal guarantee from the directors. | Operator policy in this base, common in trade credit. | A warning only. |
| product-information | A designated building product on sale has no link to its published product information. | New Zealand: Building (Building Product Information Requirements) Regulations 2022, for designated building products (structural wood-based products among them) made in or imported into New Zealand from 11 December 2023. Manufacturers and importers publish the information; merchants supplying the product check it exists and that customers can get to it. A merchant that imports directly is the importer. [Regulations](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2022/170/en/latest/), [MBIE guidance](https://www.building.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/building-code-compliance/certifications-programmes/guidance-complying-with-building-product-information-regulations-2022.pdf) | The operator marks which products are designated and which they import. |
| buyer-details | An invoice over `full_info_over_cents` (NZ$1,000 including GST by default) to an account with no address. | New Zealand: since 1 April 2023, taxable supply information for a supply over $1,000 including GST includes the recipient's name and address or another identifier (Goods and Services Tax Act 1985, taxable supply information rules). [Inland Revenue record keeping](https://ird.govt.nz/about-us/videos/webinars/gst-workshop/record-keeping) | Checks the account record only. |
| stop-list-dispatch | A delivery is booked for an account that is on stop, over its limit once open orders are counted, or has an invoice past `stop_overdue_days` (60 by default). | Operator policy in this base: goods on account wait until the account is in order. `/schedule` and `/win` refuse these unless someone overrides and is recorded. | Change the stop line in `merchant.settings`. |

To clear a finding, fix the record: swap the line to the right product, correct a wrong end use with `/set-use`, register or renew the security interest and record the dates, get the signed application, add the product information link, add the buyer's address, or hold the delivery. Never edit the rule to make a finding disappear. If a rule here is out of date, update this file and the check in a new migration together, with the date and source.

Not checked here: consented plans and specifications, spans and sizes, fixings and corrosion zones, the treatment branding on the timber itself, safe handling of treated timber offcuts, dangerous goods on the truck, heavy vehicle loading and road user rules, and Australian state building rules for termite management. Those need their own evidence.
